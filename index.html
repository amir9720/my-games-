What Is One-Line Symphony? A Relaxing Musical One-Stroke Browser Puzzle

One-Line Symphony is a free one-line drawing puzzle game online that blends classic graph-theory geometry with soothing procedural music. Instead of staring at silent black-and-white shapes, every node you connect inside our dark-mode HTML5 Canvas fires a warm harmonic chord synthesized live through the Web Audio API. Your objective is simple to learn yet deeply rewarding across all 500 progressive levels: trace every single connecting edge on the board in one unbroken stroke without lifting your finger or retracing an already lit bridge.

Designed specifically as a lightweight, no-download browser brain teaser for mobile phones, iPads, and Chromebooks, One-Line Symphony loads in under a second and saves every cleared constellation privately inside your browser's local storage. Whether you are looking for a calming anxiety-relief puzzle before bed, a 5-minute logic warm-up between classes, or a mathematical Euler path challenge, the five escalating chapters adapt cleanly from 4-node warm-ups to intricate multi-loop star graphs.

How to Solve Any One-Line Puzzle Fast (The Euler Vertex Trick)

Every stage in One-Line Symphony is a verified Eulerian trail puzzle—a classic mathematical problem first solved by Leonhard Euler in the famous Seven Bridges of Königsberg riddle. You never have to guess randomly once you learn how to count the lines meeting at each glowing orb (known as a node's degree):

Node Pattern on Board Where to Start Your Stroke Where Your Line Ends
Exactly 2 Odd Nodes (3, 5, or 7 lines attached) You must start on one of the two odd nodes. Your final edge will always finish on the other odd node.
Zero Odd Nodes (all nodes have 2, 4, or 6 lines) Start on any glowing node on the constellation. Your continuous loop returns cleanly to your starting node.
Double Golden Bridges (Ch. 3–5) Count each curved parallel bridge as 1 separate connection. Save the second parallel arc for your return sweep.

Remembering this single odd-vertex rule turns intimidating 12-node webs into effortless, flowing musical runs. Whenever you get stuck on a tricky Chapter 4 or Chapter 5 star graph, tap the Undo button to step back one note or tap Hint to illuminate the optimal next bridge in gold.

One-Line Symphony FAQ

How do I know which glowing dot to start from in a one-line puzzle?
Count how many lines connect to each node. If two nodes have an odd number of lines (like 3 or 5), you must begin your stroke on one of those two odd nodes and finish on the other. If every node has an even number of lines, you can start anywhere!
Can I play One-Line Symphony on mobile touchscreens without installing an app?
Yes. Simply drag your finger smoothly from node to node (or tap adjacent dots one by one) directly in Android Chrome or iOS Safari—zero downloads required.
Are all 500 levels in One-Line Symphony guaranteed to be solvable?
Yes. Every constellation from Level 1 to Level 500 is generated directly from a verified connected Eulerian path, ensuring a valid single-stroke solution always exists.
Is there a timer or move penalty if I take my time?
No timers and no lives system. One-Line Symphony is built for relaxing, stress-free flow—you can undo moves or reset a constellation as many times as you like.
