How to Play Infinite Zoom Hidden Object (500 Worlds)

Infinite Zoom Hidden Object uses procedural vector scaling so every nested illustration—from starry observatories and sunken coral reefs to steampunk clocktowers and cyberpunk alleys—stays crystal-clear even at 65,000× magnification. Hidden across different depths of each infinite tunnel are three unique artifacts waiting to be spotted.

  • Dive Effortlessly on Any Device: Use two-finger pinch gestures on mobile, scroll your mouse wheel on desktop, or glide the smooth Zoom Depth Slider (and holdable 🔍− / 🔍+ buttons) to travel seamlessly through every portal frame.
  • Scan the Borders & Corners: Hidden artifacts tuck themselves around bookshelves, coral branches, clouds, clock gears, and temple pillars surrounding each center portal. Tap or click directly on a matching artifact to collect it!
  • Use the Depth Radar (💡): Stuck searching a 13-layer fractal tunnel? Tap Depth Radar once to reveal the exact layer depth of an unfound treasure—or tap again to glide smoothly right to that layer and pulse a golden radar ring around it.

Game FAQ

Why doesn't the artwork get blurry even when zoomed 50,000× deep?
Unlike raster bitmap images, every single nested realm, portal arch, and artifact in Infinite Zoom Hidden Object is rendered mathematically in real-time HTML5 vector graphics at 60 FPS!
Can two artifacts hide on the same depth layer?
No—every level spreads its three hidden artifacts across three distinct depth layers so you always get a rewarding journey through the infinite artwork.
