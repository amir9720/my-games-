How to Play OSINT Geo-Sleuth (500 Geolocation Cases)

OSINT Geo-Sleuth trains your real-world open-source intelligence (OSINT) instincts across 500 illustrated photo investigations spanning 32 iconic cities around the globe. Every clue hidden inside each travel photo is grounded in genuine geography, electrical standards, currencies, traffic rules, and local culture.

  • Zoom In & Inspect Every Corner: Pinch-to-zoom, scroll, or tap the quick-focus chips (🪟 Window, ☕ Cafe Menu, 🔌 Wall Outlet, 🧾 Receipt & Wi-Fi) to magnify crisp vector details up to 4.5×.
  • Cross-Reference Multiple Clues: A single Type G British plug narrows your suspect photo to the UK, Ireland, Singapore, or the UAE—then checking the currency (£ vs S$ vs AED), temperature, and skyline outside the window pinpoints the exact city!
  • Pinpoint on the World Map: Switch to 🗺️ World Map Pin, zoom into your target continent if needed, tap to drop your red locator pin, and hit 📍 CONFIRM PIN to see your exact great-circle distance in kilometers.

Game FAQ

Are the electrical outlets, currencies, and driving sides historically accurate?
Yes! Every city in OSINT Geo-Sleuth uses its real-world socket standard (Type A/B, C/F, G, I, or D/M), actual local currency symbol, genuine left/right traffic lane, authentic license plate style, and true latitude/longitude coordinates.
How can I memorize plug types and country clues quickly?
Tap 📖 Field Guide anytime beneath the viewport to open your pocket OSINT reference sheet without losing progress—or tap 💡 Clue Intel to zoom straight onto an informative detail!
