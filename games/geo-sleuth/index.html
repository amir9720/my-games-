What Is OSINT Geo-Sleuth? A Geolocation Deduction Game Online

OSINT Geo-Sleuth is a free geolocation detective and Geo-OSINT puzzle game online that teaches real-world open-source intelligence techniques used by investigative journalists and pro GeoGuessr players—without needing heavy streaming map tiles or paid subscriptions. Each of the 500 case dossiers synthesizes a crisp street-level scene rendered live in HTML5 Canvas packed with authentic regional signatures: left-hand versus right-hand traffic lanes, painted highway line colors, country-specific license plate formats, roadside reflective bollards, sun hemisphere compass telemetry, architectural skylines, climate foliage, and genuine local street scripts.

Built as a fast, no-download geography deduction game for smartphones, tablets, and Chromebooks, OSINT Geo-Sleuth runs smoothly at 60 FPS with crisp procedural Web Audio camera shutters, radar lock-on tones, and dossier alerts. Best of all, every solved case reveals a concise debrief explaining exactly which real-world clues identified that nation—turning every 30-second round into lasting global intuition.

Real-World OSINT Geolocation Meta Cheat Sheet

In Chapters 1 and 2, full field metadata—including dialing codes and domain extensions—helps you learn the ropes. By Chapters 4 and 5, intelligence briefings arrive heavily REDACTED, forcing you to read visual street clues directly! Master these five authentic OSINT geo-meta markers:

OSINT Clue Category What to Look For in the Scene Instant Country Deductions
🚗 Driving Side & Road Lines Which lane the passing car drives in + yellow vs. white dashed centerline. Left-hand drive immediately narrows suspects to UK, Japan, Australia, South Africa, India, or Thailand! Yellow outer / white center points strongly to South Africa or Norway/Americas.
🪪 License Plate Geometry Blue EU strip on left, twin blue bands, yellow rear plate, or square tall plate. Yellow rear plate = UK or Netherlands; Dual blue strips (left & right) = Italy or France; Green/Yellow square plate = Japan; Blue top bar = Brazil Mercosur!
🪧 Roadside Bollards White/red posts, orange Nordic snow sticks, or wrap-around red reflectors. Orange tall snow poles = Norway/Iceland; White post with red band + white tip = Australia; Black-cap slanted bar = Germany/Poland!
🔤 Unique Alphabet Letters Distinctive diacritics and scripts on shop awnings and highway boards. ß = Germany; Å/Ø = Norway; Ä/Ö + Å = Sweden; Ł/Ż = Poland; Ğ/Ş = Turkey; 가/한 = South Korea; ก/ข = Thailand; Ω/Δ = Greece!
☀️ Sun & Hemisphere Check the compass HUD badge (`SUN: SOUTH` vs `SUN: NORTH`). Sun due NORTH = Southern Hemisphere (Australia, Brazil, South Africa, Argentina)! Sun due SOUTH = Northern Hemisphere!

Investigator Pro Tip: Whenever two European or Asian countries share similar scripts or plates, cross-check two clues together—for example, both Sweden and Norway sit in northern pine forests with right-hand traffic, yet Norway uses Ø/Æ while Sweden uses Ö/Ä and distinctive blue-background highway signs! OSINT Geo-Sleuth FAQ

How does the Sun Compass clue (`SUN: NORTH` vs `SUN: SOUTH`) help identify the country?
In the Northern Hemisphere (Europe, North America, East Asia), the midday sun arcs across the Southern sky. In the Southern Hemisphere (Australia, Brazil, Argentina, South Africa), the midday sun shines from the North—instantly eliminating half the globe!
Why are some Evidence Cards marked "[REDACTED]" in Chapters 2 through 5?
As your investigator rank climbs, easy giveaways like international calling codes (`+81`, `+46`) and web domains (`.de`, `.br`) are redacted so you practice genuine visual OSINT—identifying countries from driving side, license plate design, roadside bollards, street script, and climate!
What happens if I select the wrong country suspect?
You have 3 Attempts per case. A wrong guess crosses that suspect off the board and gives a brief clue nudge (like hemisphere or driving side) so you can narrow down the real location logically!
Does OSINT Geo-Sleuth require Google Maps or heavy image downloads?
Zero external maps or megabytes of images! Every street view scene, passing car, license plate, bollard, and storefront sign is drawn procedurally in crisp HTML5 Canvas—working smoothly even on slow Wi-Fi or school Chromebooks.
