How to Play Grocery Bagging Physics (500 Orders)

Grocery Bagging Physics turns everyday supermarket checkout into a hilarious 2D Matter.js engineering puzzle. Instead of forcing a random sequence, all items sit right on the Checkout Counter Tray—you choose which item goes into the brown kraft paper bag first, aim its position, rotate it cleanly, and pack everything beneath the green rim line.

  • Rule 1 — Heavy & Sturdy First: Always pack Watermelons 🍉, Soup Cans 🥫, Milk Cartons 🥛, and Cereal Boxes 📦 at the bottom of the bag to form a flat, solid foundation.
  • Rule 2 — Protect Fragile Goods: Eggs 🥚, Chips 🍟, and Soft Bread 🍞 belong strictly on the upper layer! Dropping or resting heavy items on top of them builds red crush stress until they crack or pop.
  • Rule 3 — Shield the Paper Walls from Cacti 🌵: Starting in Chapter 3, customers buy potted cacti! The reinforced cardboard floor at the bottom is puncture-proof, but if a cactus touches the thin left or right brown paper walls (or pokes a fragile item), it tears the bag instantly. Wedge sturdy cartons against the walls first, then tuck the cactus safely in the middle!

Game FAQ

Can I change which grocery item I pack next?
Yes! Tap any item on the Checkout Counter tray below the bag at any time to switch what's in your hand before dropping it.
What does the Packing Hint (💡) button do?
Packing Hint selects the smartest next item based on weight and fragility, levels its rotation, and highlights a recommended golden drop lane above the bag.
