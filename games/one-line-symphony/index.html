How to Play One-Line Symphony

One-Line Symphony blends classic graph-tracing brain teasers with soothing interactive sound design. Every stage presents a glowing constellation made of nodes and faint paths. Your goal is to light up every single path in one continuous line without passing over the same segment twice.

  • Touch or Mouse Controls: Press on any glowing star and drag your finger or mouse along connected paths. Release anytime to pause, then continue dragging from the pulsing star—or drag backward to undo a step.
  • Musical Feedback: Every star triggers a warm pentatonic harp note synthesized directly in your browser using the Web Audio API. Finishing a constellation replays your entire path as a short melody.
  • Pro Strategy Tip: Look closely at how many lines meet at each star. If a puzzle has two stars with an odd number of paths (like 3 or 5), you must always start at one of those odd stars and finish at the other!

Game FAQ

Are all levels in One-Line Symphony solvable?
Yes. Every constellation is mathematically verified to have either zero or two odd-degree nodes, guaranteeing at least one unbroken solution path.
Why don't I hear sound right away?
Mobile and desktop browsers activate audio only after your first tap or click. Touch any star on the board and make sure the Sound toggle is set to ON.
