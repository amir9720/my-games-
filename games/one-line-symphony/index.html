How to Play One-Line Symphony (500 Levels)

One-Line Symphony combines classic Eulerian graph-tracing logic with live procedural harp audio. Across 5 musical chapters—Dawn Prelude, Starlight Etude, Nebula Nocturne, Solaris Sonata, and Cosmic Symphony—you will trace 500 unique constellations in a single unbroken line without crossing the same segment twice.

  • Mobile & Mouse Controls: Touch any star and drag along connected paths. You can lift your finger anytime to pause, drag backward to undo a line, or tap the Undo button.
  • Using Hints: Stuck on a tough chapter? Tap Hint to illuminate a valid starting star or the exact next line in golden light.
  • The Secret Rule of One-Line Puzzles: Count how many lines connect to each star. If a constellation has two stars with an odd number of paths (3, 5, or 7), your stroke must always begin at one odd star and finish at the other!

Game FAQ

Are all 500 levels mathematically solvable?
Yes. Every single level from 1 to 500 is deterministically constructed from a verified Eulerian path, guaranteeing at least one unbroken solution.
Does the game save my progress across all 500 levels?
Yes. Your solved puzzles and current level are saved automatically in your browser's local storage so you can continue anytime.
