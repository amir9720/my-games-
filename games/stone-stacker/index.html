How to Play Gravity Stone Stacker (500 Levels)

Gravity Stone Stacker is a pure 2D rigid-body physics puzzle powered by Matter.js. Each stage hands you between 5 and 10 irregular geometric stones—flat slabs, wedges, pentagons, and heavy basalt chunks. Your mission is simple to understand and thrilling to master: stack every stone onto the stone pedestal and keep the tower balanced for 5 full seconds without a single piece tumbling off.

  • Mobile & Desktop Controls: Drag left or right anywhere on the sky to aim your hovering stone. Tap ⟲ -15° or ⟳ +15° (or press Q/E on a keyboard) to align its flat edge, then tap DROP STONE (or press Space) to release it gently.
  • Watch Stone Weight & Wind: Darker volcanic basalt stones are significantly heavier than pale sandstone blocks—use heavy stones near the base or to counterbalance a leaning stack. Starting in Chapter 4, mountain crosswinds push lightly against tall towers!
  • Use the Balance Guide (💡): Tap Balance Guide to auto-level your current stone's flattest face downward and reveal your tower's live golden center-of-gravity plumb line.

Game FAQ

Why does the camera move upward while I stack?
As your tower grows taller across 8-to-10-stone levels, the camera smoothly glides upward so your drop zone always stays comfortably above the highest stone without going off screen.
When does the 5-second countdown begin?
The 5.0-second stability timer starts as soon as your final stone touches the tower. If every stone stays balanced above the abyss until the timer hits 0.0s, you clear the level!
