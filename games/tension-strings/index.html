What Is Tension & Strings? A Tactile Untangle Line Puzzle Online

Tension & Strings is a free untangle lines browser game inspired by the classic mathematical puzzle Planarity, upgraded with vibrating elastic string physics and live harp-like Web Audio. Every puzzle presents a tangled network of luminous nodes wired together by glowing elastic filaments. Whenever two lines cross over each other, both strings flash neon crimson and spark at the intersection point. Drag nodes freely around the canvas until zero lines intersect and the entire geometric web turns calm emerald green.

Engineered as a lightweight, no-download spatial brain teaser for smartphones, tablets, and Chromebooks, Tension & Strings runs smoothly at 60 FPS with generous touch hitboxes and zero timers. Every release plucks the attached filaments with soft synthesized acoustic tones, turning logical untangling into an oddly satisfying sensory break you can play for two minutes or two hours.

How to Untangle Complex Planar Graphs Quickly (Pro Strategy Table)

Early levels with 6 or 7 nodes untangle intuitively in seconds, but once you reach 16-node lattices in Chapters 4 and 5, dragging randomly can feel chaotic. Use these four proven planar graph untangling techniques to flatten even the densest knots methodically:

Untangling Technique What to Look For How to Execute It
1. Pull Out Low-Degree Nodes First Nodes connected to only 2 or 3 strings. Drag them out toward the perimeter of the board first—they naturally form clean outer triangle edges.
2. Spot Closed Triangles Three mutually linked nodes forming a tight 3-sided loop. Keep triangle neighbors positioned close together so their three filaments turn green as a solid building block.
3. Isolate High-Degree Hubs Busy central dots with 5 or more strings attached. Drag the hub near the center of the board and fan its connected neighbors radially around it like bicycle spokes.
4. Build Around Gold Anchors Locked hexagonal pins (Chapters 3–5) that cannot move. Trace which movable nodes connect directly to each Gold Anchor and place them in the same quadrant immediately!

Still facing one stubborn crossing? Tap 💡 Smart Hint—our solver identifies the single node causing the highest number of intersections and lights up a pulsing golden target ring showing a verified crossing-free destination for it.

Tension & Strings FAQ

Are all 500 levels in Tension & Strings mathematically guaranteed to have a zero-crossing solution?
Yes! Every stage is constructed from an exact non-crossing planar triangulation before its movable nodes are scrambled, guaranteeing at least one clean zero-intersection layout always exists.
Why can't I drag the hexagonal Gold Anchor Pins in Chapters 3 through 5?
Gold Anchor Pins are locked stationary checkpoints already positioned in a valid planar spot. Your challenge is to untangle and arrange the movable blue nodes cleanly around those fixed anchors!
Is there only one exact position for each node to solve the level?
Not at all! Any arrangement of the nodes where zero strings overlap wins the level immediately—even if your untangled shape is mirrored, rotated, or stretched completely differently from the Hint layout.
Does Tension & Strings work smoothly with touchscreens on Android and iPhone?
Yes. Touch dragging uses expanded fingertip hitboxes and pointer lock so moving nodes feels effortless on smartphones and tablets without scrolling the page.
