How to Play Tension & Strings (500 Levels)

Tension & Strings is a calming spatial-reasoning puzzle inspired by planar graph geometry. Every stage starts with a tangled web of elastic strings connecting glowing nodes. Any string that crosses another glows red, while completely free strings shine emerald green. Drag the nodes around the board until the crossing counter hits zero.

  • Start from the Outer Frame: Pull nodes that have only 2 or 3 connections out toward the corners first. Opening up the perimeter creates wide space in the center to untangle denser inner triangles.
  • Watch Node Highlights: While dragging a node, all of its attached strings brighten so you can immediately see which lines are pulling across the web.
  • Use the Untangle Hint (💡): Stuck in a 20-node Gordian knot? Tap Untangle 1 Node to smoothly glide a tangled node directly to its verified geometric coordinate and mark it with a golden ring.

Game FAQ

Is every level guaranteed to have a zero-crossing solution?
Yes. All 500 levels are generated as mathematically planar graphs first—ensuring a completely non-overlapping geometric layout exists—before scrambling the node positions.
Do I have to match one exact shape to win?
No! Any arrangement of the nodes where zero strings intersect wins the level immediately, allowing you to discover your own clean geometric solution.
