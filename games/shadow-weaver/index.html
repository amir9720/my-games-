How to Play Shadow Weaver (500 3D Shadow Puzzles)

Shadow Weaver is a meditative spatial-perception puzzle inspired by theatrical shadowplay. Floating inside a warm gallery spotlight is an abstract 3D sculpture built from carved wood and brass blocks scattered in depth. From most angles it looks completely unrecognizable—until you rotate it into the exact hidden angle where every piece aligns to cast a crisp, familiar silhouette onto the illuminated wall above.

  • Intuitive 3D Rotation: Drag left, right, up, or down anywhere on the gallery stage (or use Arrow keys / WASD on desktop) to spin the 3D sculpture freely while watching its shadow morph in real time on the wall.
  • Unlock 3-Axis Roll & Twin Sculptures: Chapters 1 and 2 focus on smooth 2-axis discovery. Starting in Chapter 3 (Level 201), the Roll (⟲ / ⟳) dial unlocks full 3-axis alignment. Chapters 4 and 5 (Levels 301–500) suspend two separate sculptures side-by-side—switch between Sculpture A and B to compose paired shadow scenes!
  • Watch the Alignment Bar & Listen: As your cast shadow approaches the true silhouette, the Alignment Bar rises smoothly toward 100%, the wall warms with golden light, and soft harmonic notes chime to let you know you're getting close.

Game FAQ

How does the Shadow Hint (💡) button help without spoiling the fun?
The first tap projects a faint golden chalk outline of the target silhouette onto the gallery wall. Tapping Shadow Hint again smoothly glides the active sculpture halfway closer to its solved orientation!
Why do symmetric shapes like hearts or pine trees snap into place from either side?
Shadow Weaver measures real-time pixel-mask silhouette overlap (IoU) on the wall—so any 3D orientation that produces the exact same recognizable shadow is accepted cleanly!
