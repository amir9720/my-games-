What Is Shadow Weaver? A Relaxing 3D Shadow Silhouette Puzzle Online

Shadow Weaver is a free 3D shadow silhouette browser game inspired by beloved spatial-art experiences like Shadowmatic, engineered to run instantaneously inside any mobile or desktop web browser with zero downloads. Each exhibit suspends an abstract cluster of carved wooden blocks above a warm studio spotlight. From random viewing angles the floating fragments appear completely unrelated—yet when rotated into the exact orthographic perspective, their cast shadows fuse together on the gallery wall to reveal a crisp silhouette such as a sailboat, vintage key, crescent moon, rocket ship, royal crown, or acoustic guitar.

Built as a calm, no-download spatial perception puzzle for smartphones, iPads, and Chromebooks, Shadow Weaver features zero countdown timers, soothing wooden chime feedback via the Web Audio API, and a real-time 6-dot Resonance Alignment Meter that glows progressively brighter as you get warmer—turning abstract 3D visualization into a meditative art break.

How to Read 3D Shadows Like a Sculptor (Mastery Guide)

Rotating multi-axis 3D sculptures in Chapters 2 through 5 can feel wonderfully tricky at first. Use these four visual perception techniques to lock onto hidden shapes smoothly without guessing blindly:

Chapter & Control Mode Key Visual Cue Best Alignment Strategy
Chapter 1: Single-Axis Spin (Levels 1–100) Pieces spread horizontally when off-angle and compress cleanly at the sweet spot. Sweep slowly left or right while watching the Focus % and golden resonance dots light up!
Chapter 2: 2-Axis Free Orbit (Levels 101–200) Flat wooden slabs appear razor-thin when viewed edge-on. First lock horizontal spin until vertical edges stack neatly, then tilt smoothly up or down to refine pitch.
Chapter 3: Twin Sculptures (Levels 201–300) Piece A (warm walnut) and Piece B (golden maple) combine into one unified shadow. Align Piece A first until its button turns green (✓), then switch to Piece B to finish the composite artwork!
Chapters 4–5: Full 3-Axis + Roll (Levels 301–500) Shape looks recognizable on the wall, yet tilted sideways or upside-down. Once the shadow outline looks sharp, tap ⟲ Roll or ⟳ Roll (or press Q/E) to level it upright!

Curious what shape is hiding inside the abstract timber cluster? Check the Category Clue in the top-right bar (such as Sky & Space, Nature, or Everyday Objects)—or tap 💡 Shadow Hint to briefly project a golden dotted silhouette outline onto the gallery wall and nudge the sculpture closer to focus.

Shadow Weaver FAQ

How do I know if I'm getting closer to the hidden shadow shape?
Watch the 6 golden Resonance Dots and the Focus % pill above the gallery wall! As you rotate toward the true viewing angle, more dots light up in warm gold and the wall shadow sharpens.
How do Twin Sculptures (Piece A & Piece B) work in Chapters 3 and 5?
Some exhibits split a hidden silhouette across two separate wooden clusters! Tap 🪵 Piece A or 🪵 Piece B beneath the spotlight to switch which cluster you are rotating—as soon as one piece locks near its sweet spot, its button highlights green so you can finish the second piece!
What does the Shadow Hint (💡) button show?
Tapping Shadow Hint flashes a faint golden dotted silhouette blueprint on the gallery wall for three seconds and gently glides the active wooden piece partway toward its correct 3D angle.
Can I play Shadow Weaver on Android, iPhone, and Chromebook?
Yes! Simply drag anywhere on the canvas with your finger or mouse to spin the 3D wooden sculpture smoothly at 60 FPS—plus use Q and E on desktop keyboards for quick roll turns in Chapters 4–5.
