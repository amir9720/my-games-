What Is Micro-Expression Polygraph? A Lie Detector Detective Game Online

Micro-Expression Polygraph is a free facial micro-expression and lie detector game online inspired by behavioral psychology classics like L.A. Noire and Paul Ekman's Facial Action Coding System (FACS). Seated beneath a single swinging interrogation lamp is a live animated suspect wired to a dual-channel biometric polygraph measuring both Heart Rate (BPM) and Galvanic Skin Response (GSR). As your detective asks 3 to 5 pointed questions across each case, you must watch the suspect's face and scrolling ink needles in real time to separate genuine truth from carefully rehearsed deception.

Built as a rich, no-download psychological mystery game for smartphones, iPads, and Chromebooks, Micro-Expression Polygraph features procedural 60 FPS facial rigging—blinking eyes, shifting pupils, twitching brows, lip compression, cold sweat beads, and fleeting "duping delight" smirks—paired with live Web Audio heartbeat pulses, mechanical polygraph pen scratches, tape slow-motion clicks, and courtroom gavel chimes across all 500 interrogation cases.

Forensic Behavioral Reading Guide (How to Catch Every Lie)

Anyone can spot an obvious panic in Chapter 1—but trained liars in Chapters 4 and 5 flash micro-expressions lasting under half a second, while innocent suspects sometimes exhibit harmless nervous habits! Master these six behavioral tells and always cross-check the top Suspect Baseline Bar:

Deceptive Micro-Expression What to Spot on the Face Polygraph Needle Signature
😏 Duping Delight (Micro-Smirk) One corner of the mouth pulls sharply upward for a split second mid-answer! Sharp crimson GSR spike + brief +18 BPM surge.
👀 Hard Lateral Eye-Dart Both pupils snap sideways away from the detective before snapping back. Double jagged BPM pulse + sudden cyan tension tremor.
🤨 Asymmetric Brow Twitch Inner eyebrow lifts or pinches unevenly (micro-fear / guilt flash). Tall sharp GSR deflection rising cleanly above baseline.
😬 Tight Lip Compression Lips press flat into a thin rigid line while hiding withheld details. Elevated sustained Heart Rate + GSR plateau.
💧 Cold Sweat & Pupil Dilation Tiny glistening sweat bead slips down the temple as pupils widen sharply. Heavy unmistakable twin-needle Polygraph Spike.
⚠️ Innocent Baseline Quirks (Ch. 3–5) Check the blue HABIT chip above the tape! E.g. Frequent Blinker or Naturally High 88 BPM. Do NOT mistake a baseline habit for a lie! Wait for an actual micro-tell + spike ABOVE their baseline!

Interrogator Pro Tip: Never rush your verdict on a fast flash! Tap ⏪ Replay 0.5× Slow-Mo (or press R on desktop) to rewind the current statement and watch the suspect's eyes, brows, and mouth at half speed with a live SLOW-MO telemetry overlay—even a 0.35-second poker-face twitch in Chapter 5 becomes crystal clear in slow motion!

Micro-Expression Polygraph FAQ

How do I spot a super-fast micro-expression in Chapters 4 and 5?
Tap ⏪ Replay 0.5× Slow-Mo right below the interrogation transcript! The tape plays the exact statement back at half speed, stretching every 0.35-second eye dart, brow twitch, or lip smirk so you can verify it clearly before hitting ✅ TRUTH or 🚨 LIE.
Why did an innocent suspect have 90 BPM or blink rapidly when telling the truth?
Check the blue HABIT badge in the Suspect Baseline Bar! Starting in Chapter 3, innocent suspects can have a naturally high resting heart rate or frequent blinking habit—look for a sudden spike above baseline paired with a deceptive facial micro-tell rather than resting nerves!
What does the "💡 Behavioral Scan" hint button show?
Tapping 💡 Behavioral Scan runs an automated forensic telemetry readout on the current statement, telling you immediately whether a deceptive facial tell / GSR anomaly was detected—or if readings stayed cleanly at baseline!
Are keyboard shortcuts supported on desktop and Chromebooks?
Yes! Press T (or Left Arrow) for TRUTH, L (or Right Arrow) for LIE, and R (or Space) to replay the current statement in 0.5× Slow-Mo!
