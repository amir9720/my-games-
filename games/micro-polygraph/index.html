How to Play Micro-Expression Polygraph (500 Cases)

Micro-Expression Polygraph puts you behind the one-way mirror of a noir detective bureau across 500 escalating interrogations. Each suspect answers a series of sharp questions while hooked up to a live ECG polygraph strip. Genuine deception leaks out in brief involuntary facial tells lasting a fraction of a second:

  • Learn the 4 True Deception Tells: Keep your eyes locked on the suspect's face as their statement plays out. A Rapid Eyelid Twitch, a sudden sideways Eye Dart, a one-sided Micro-Smirk (Duping Delight), or an involuntary Inner Eyebrow Pinch always reveals a lie!
  • Don't Be Fooled by Nervous Innocents (Chapter 2+): Innocent witnesses can sweat nervously or experience a mild heart-rate bump under the hot interrogation lamp—but their eyes, brows, and lips show zero deception tells. Judge them as ✓ TRUTH!
  • Counter Sunglasses & Trained Spies (Chapters 3–5): High-tier suspects wear dark glasses to mask their eyes or train their breathing to hold a flat polygraph ECG line. Watch their brows and mouth carefully, and use ⏪ 0.45× Slow-Mo Replay whenever a statement flashes by too fast!

Game FAQ

What is the difference between a normal blink and a deception eyelid twitch?
A normal blink closes both eyes smoothly and symmetrically for a quick beat. A deception Eyelid Twitch causes a rapid, asymmetric flutter on a single eye (often accompanied by a subtle tension flash)!
How does the Tell Scanner (💡) button help?
Tapping Tell Scanner replays the current answer in slow motion with a glowing golden forensic scan frame locked directly over the facial region where a micro-expression occurs—or alerts you if the baseline scan is clean.
