What Is Rube Goldberg Sandbox? A Chain-Reaction Physics Game Online

Rube Goldberg Sandbox is a free contraption builder physics game online inspired by classic workshop inventions like The Incredible Machine and Bad Piggies, engineered directly in lightweight HTML5 Canvas and Matter.js so you can tinker right inside any mobile phone, tablet, or Chromebook browser with zero downloads. Every blueprint drops a polished steel marble out of an overhead brass launcher pipe. Standing between the marble and the gleaming Golden Goal Bell are riveted steel girders, deep shafts, and wide horizontal chasms—and your job is to position and angle your movable workshop gadgets to guide the ball smoothly to victory.

Best of all, Rube Goldberg Sandbox separates EDIT Mode from RUN Mode cleanly: press ▶ RUN MACHINE to test your invention live, watch the glowing marble trajectory trail, and tap ↺ EDIT & TWEAK anytime to adjust a single ramp angle by 15 degrees without ever resetting the rest of your build! Every solved machine across all 500 blueprints saves automatically inside private local storage.

Rube Goldberg Sandbox FAQ

Do I have to place my gadgets in the exact Blueprint Hint positions to win?
Not at all! Rube Goldberg Sandbox runs real live 2D physics—any creative arrangement of your ramps, springs, fans, and magnetic boosters that guides the steel marble into the Golden Bell wins immediately!
Will pressing Run or Edit erase the contraption I already built?
Never! Tapping ↺ EDIT & TWEAK returns the steel marble to the launcher pipe while keeping every single gadget right where you placed it—plus leaves a faint cyan trail of your last run so fine-tuning takes seconds.
How do I rotate a wooden ramp, fan, or magnetic booster on mobile?
Tap or drag any gadget on the canvas to highlight it with a glowing golden ring, then tap ⟲ −15° or +15° ⟳ below the board (or press Q / E on desktop keyboards) to aim its trajectory arrow precisely!
Can I play Rube Goldberg Sandbox on school Chromebooks and smartphones without installing?
Yes. All 500 physics machines run 100% client-side in HTML5 Canvas at 60 FPS with zero downloads, zero accounts, and automatic private local storage saving.
