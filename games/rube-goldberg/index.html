How to Play Rube Goldberg Sandbox (500 Machines)

Rube Goldberg Sandbox combines open-ended mechanical engineering with real-time 2D Matter.js rigid-body physics across 500 escalating contraptions—plus a full Free Sandbox mode. Select gadgets from your workbench tray, position and angle them anywhere on the blueprint grid, and press ▶ RUN MACHINE to drop the steel ball into motion.

  • Master All 4 Contraption Gadgets: Use 📐 Ramps to build speed and glide over obstacles, 🌀 Spring Pads to launch the ball skyward with high-energy rebounds, 💨 Turbine Fans (Chapter 2+) to blow the ball through vertical air shafts or across wide canyons, and 🧲 Electromagnets (Chapter 3+) set to PULL or PUSH to bend the ball's orbit around ceilings and ledges.
  • Use Your Dotted Flight Trail: Every test run leaves behind a crisp cyan dotted trail showing the ball's exact trajectory. Tap ■ STOP & EDIT anytime—all your gadgets stay in place so you can tweak a ramp by 15° or nudge a spring pad in a single second!
  • Unlock Laser Gates & Pivot Seesaws (Chapters 4–5): High-tier blueprints guard the Goal Bell behind a red laser seal and introduce live pivoting seesaws. Route your ball to bump the golden ◈ Trigger Switch first to drop the barrier, then arc into the basket!

Game FAQ

What can I do in Free Sandbox (🛠️) mode?
Tapping 🛠️ Sandbox unlocks unlimited Ramps, Springs, Fans, and Magnets on an open blueprint—and lets you drag both the Start Dropper and Goal Basket anywhere on the canvas to invent your own wild chain reactions!
How does the Blueprint Hint (💡) help?
Blueprint Hint projects subtle golden ghost outlines on the stage showing a recommended gadget arrangement and angle to get the steel ball smoothly around the obstacles toward the goal.
