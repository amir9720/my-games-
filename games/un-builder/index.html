What Is Un-Builder? A Mechanical Disassembly & Screw Puzzle Online

Un-Builder is a free mechanical disassembly puzzle game online crafted for anyone who has ever wondered what sits inside a vintage cassette player, brass alarm clock, or classic SLR camera. Spread across an anti-static blue ESD workbench are 500 layered retro devices. Just like a real repair teardown, every metal shield, ribbon cable, copper coil, and circuit board is physically locked down by fasteners or brackets sitting above it—meaning you must pick the matching tool and strip each machine strictly from the outside in.

Engineered as a lightweight, no-download reverse-engineering brain teaser for phones, tablets, and Chromebooks, Un-Builder runs at a smooth 60 FPS with live procedural Web Audio ratchet clicks, screwdriver spins, and metallic clunks. There are no harsh timers or paywalled toolkits—just calm, methodical mechanical deduction and a 3-star clean-teardown score for zero-jam precision.

Workbench Tool Belt & Zero-Jam Teardown Strategy

To earn a ⭐⭐⭐ Zero-Jam Teardown on multi-layer prototypes, match each hardware type to its exact tool and inspect physical overlaps before pulling a panel:

Workbench Tool Target Hardware When to Use It in the Sequence
🪛 Driver (Ch. 1+) Chrome Phillips & slotted chassis screws Always clear outer faceplate screws first, then watch for hidden inner board screws underneath covers!
✂️ Unclip (Ch. 2+) Colored power wires, ribbon leads & pink safety latches Disconnect exterior plastic latches early, and always unclip internal wires linking two PCBs before lifting either module.
🔧 Wrench (Ch. 3+) Heavy golden M6 hex bolts & inner anchor nuts Unbolt cross-locking steel brackets first—those brackets physically trap multiple shield covers beneath them.
🧤 Lift (Ch. 1+) Steel brackets, access covers, PCBs, gears, coils & lenses Pull away completely unfastened layers from top to bottom until the stamped chassis reads EMPTY!

Pro Tip: If you accidentally tap a locked plate or circuit board too early, watch the flashing red-orange dashed dependency lines—they point straight to the exact screw, wire, or cross-bracket still holding that piece down! Or tap 💡 Next Part anytime to spotlight a guaranteed free component in gold and auto-select its matching tool.

Un-Builder FAQ

Are all 500 retro devices guaranteed to be 100% disassemblable without deadlocks?
Yes! Every device is assembled programmatically from Layer 0 (deep core modules) outward to Layer 4 (surface fasteners), forming a strict acyclic dependency tree so at least one completely free part is always accessible.
Why did a cover plate clunk and flash red lines when I tapped it?
That means another fastener, cross-bracket, or clip is still mechanically locking that plate down! Follow the glowing red dashed lines to see the exact screws or brackets you need to remove first.
What happens if I tap a free screw while holding the wrong tool?
No jam penalty is added for holding the wrong tool—Un-Builder simply highlights the required tool on your bottom belt in glowing gold so you can switch with one quick tap!
Does Un-Builder work smoothly on Android phones, iPhones, and Chromebooks?
Yes. Every screw, hex nut, wire, and latch uses generous mobile touch hitboxes and crisp top-down layer prioritization so small fasteners are easy to unscrew without zooming or app downloads.
