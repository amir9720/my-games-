What Is Minimalist Logic Gates? A Boolean Circuit Puzzle Game Online

Minimalist Logic Gates is a free online logic gate puzzle game that turns digital computer science fundamentals into a sleek, tactile circuit-building experience. Every board feeds fixed binary input pins (1 HIGH in glowing electric cyan and 0 LOW in muted slate) through cascading layers of open microchip sockets toward one or more target output LEDs on the right. Your job is to select the right Boolean logic chips from your workbench tray and snap them into the board until every target LED lights up to match its exact required state (REQ 1 or REQ 0).

Built as a lightweight, no-download STEM brain teaser for students, programmers, and Chromebook users, Minimalist Logic Gates evaluates every trace instantaneously at 60 FPS with crisp electronic relay audio via the Web Audio API. There are zero timers, zero paywalls, and automatic private progress saving across all 500 verified circuits.

Complete Boolean Logic Gate Truth Table Cheat Sheet

New to digital electronics or brushing up for a computer science class? Keep this quick 6-Gate Truth Table Reference handy as you unlock XOR in Chapter 2 and universal NAND / NOR gates in Chapter 3:

Gate Chip Inputs When Output Equals 1 (HIGH) Quick Mental Shortcut
AND 2 Pins Only when BOTH inputs are 1 (1 & 1 → 1) Strict agreement—if either wire is 0, output stays 0.
OR 2 Pins When AT LEAST ONE input is 1 (1|0, 0|1, 1|1 → 1) Only outputs 0 when both wires are 0.
NOT 1 Pin Only when its single input is 0 (0 → 1, 1 → 0) Pure inverter—flips whatever signal enters it!
XOR 2 Pins When inputs are DIFFERENT (1^0 → 1, 0^1 → 1) "Exclusive OR"—outputs 0 if both inputs match (0,0 or 1,1).
NAND 2 Pins Always 1 EXCEPT when both inputs are 1 (1&1 → 0) Exact opposite of AND (inverted AND gate).
NOR 2 Pins Only when BOTH inputs are 0 (0,0 → 1) Exact opposite of OR (inverted OR gate).

Circuit Engineer Tip: Notice the shape of each empty socket on the PCB! Compact 1-pin triangular sockets accept single-input chips (NOT or straight PASS buffers), whereas wider 2-pin rectangular sockets accept dual-input chips (AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR).

Minimalist Logic Gates FAQ

How do I place or remove a logic gate chip on the circuit board?
Tap any available gate chip (such as AND, OR, or NOT) in the bottom tray to select it, then tap an empty socket on the board to snap it in. To remove a placed gate and return it to your tray, simply tap that socket again!
Why do some circuits have both REQ 1 and REQ 0 target LEDs?
In Chapters 4 and 5, a circuit is only solved when every target LED matches its label simultaneously—meaning a REQ 1 LED must receive a glowing cyan 1 signal while a REQ 0 LED must receive a dark 0 signal!
Is this game helpful for learning computer science and digital electronics?
Yes! Seeing live 1 and 0 pulses propagate through AND, OR, NOT, XOR, NAND, and NOR gates builds intuitive Boolean reasoning used in programming, FPGA design, and discrete math.
Does Minimalist Logic Gates work on Chromebooks and mobile phones without login?
Yes. All 500 circuit levels run 100% client-side in HTML5 Canvas with zero downloads, and your solved progress saves automatically in your browser's local storage.
