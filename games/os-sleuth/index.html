How to Play OS Sleuth (500 Detective Cases)

OS Sleuth turns your browser into a seized 1998 desktop computer left behind by a missing person. Across 500 escalating forensic cases, you must comb through offline emails in Inbox 98, personal logs in My Documents, and erased drafts in the Recycle Bin to crack password-protected ZIP vaults and uncover the real story.

  • Step 1 — Gather Password Clues: Owners rarely write down passwords in one obvious place. In Chapter 1, look for a direct hint in a deleted note or email. In Chapters 2–5, combine two scattered clues—or follow coded rules like reversing a codeword—to unlock nested ZIP archives.
  • Step 2 — Watch for Decoys: Not every email is the truth! Cancelled flight confirmations, casual coworker chatter, and outdated drafts act as red herrings. Always verify your conclusions inside the decrypted ZIP file.
  • Step 3 — File the Case Report: Open Case Report on the desktop (or via the Start menu), select the verified contact, destination, and critical evidence item, and click Submit Official Report to close the case.

Game FAQ

Are ZIP passwords case-sensitive?
No. You can type passwords in uppercase or lowercase—the vault automatically trims extra spaces and checks your code cleanly.
What happens in Chapters 4 and 5 (Cases 301–500)?
Higher chapters introduce dual-layer encryption: cracking the first ZIP file reveals a hidden memo with the rule needed to unlock the second vault containing the final truth.
