What Is OS Sleuth? A Desktop Mystery & Digital Forensics Game Online

OS Sleuth is a free desktop computer detective game online inspired by acclaimed investigative mysteries like Cyber Manhunt, Her Story, and Emily Is Away—engineered to run smoothly inside any mobile, tablet, or Chromebook browser with zero downloads. Each of the 500 forensic cases drops you into a seized corporate or institutional workstation moments after a major security breach: a midnight vault blueprint leak, an unauthorized payroll transfer, a prototype chip theft, or a scrubbed server backup.

Rather than feeding you passive multiple-choice trivia, OS Sleuth hands you a live five-app desktop environment (`ZinOS Forensics`) packed with authentic digital breadcrumbs. You'll cross-check staff chat alibis against raw Badge & VPN System Logs, rescue shredded audit files from the Trash Bin, deduce 4-digit PINs from sticky notes to crack Encrypted Vaults, and spin the built-in Caesar & Reverse Cipher Decoder before issuing your arrest warrant in the ⚖️ Accuse terminal!

Digital Forensics Investigator's Playbook (All 5 Workstation Apps)

Great cyber sleuths never guess—they follow the metadata! Learn how each built-in workstation app connects to expose the guilty insider cleanly across Chapters 1 through 5:

Workstation App Evidence Stored Inside Key Forensic Technique
📧 Mail & Chat Internal emails, IT security memos, direct chat alibis, and staff phone extensions. Look for alibi contradictions (someone claiming they left early) and subtle hints for cracking the Vault PIN (like reversed extensions or incident room numbers)!
📁 Files & Vault Sticky notes, HR staff memos, a recoverable 🗑️ Trash Bin (Ch. 3+), and a 🔒 PIN-Locked Vault (Ch. 2+). Always tap ♻️ Restore Deleted File when a shredded log appears in Trash, and enter the deduced 4-digit PIN to decrypt the smoking-gun vault file!
🖥️ System Logs Timestamped Door Badge swipes, USB mount records, VPN logins, and Server Backup times. Compare the red CRITICAL breach timestamp against every suspect's Badge ID, Workstation IP, and chat alibi—logs never lie, even when suspects do!
🔐 Cipher Decoder (Ch. 4–5) Interactive Caesar Shift (-25 to +25) and Reverse Text forensic decryptor. Spot scrambled text inside a vault or chat? Tap 📤 Send to Decoder and step the shift buttons until the hidden codename or badge pops into clear English!
⚖️ Accuse Terminal Suspect roster displaying each person's Name, Role, Badge ID, Desk/Ext, and Official Alibi. You have 2 Arrest Warrants per case—verify both Badge ID and evidence trail before locking in your accusation!

Cyber Sleuth Pro Tip: Notice a glowing amber dot on the 📁 Files/Vault or 🔐 Decoder dock icon? That badge alerts you that an unopened encrypted vault or undecoded cipher is still waiting inside! Stuck connecting two clues on a complex Chapter 5 breach? Tap 💡 Forensic Lead for a progressive step-by-step investigator nudge (first pointing to the right app, then revealing the exact vault PIN or contradiction).

OS Sleuth FAQ

How do I figure out the 4-digit PIN for the Encrypted Vault in Chapters 2–5?
Check the Sticky Note inside 📁 Files/Vault or the IT Security memo in 📧 Mail & Chat! It always tells you the exact formula—such as the 4-digit midnight backup timestamp found in 🖥️ Sys Logs, or an admin's phone extension typed backward!
How do I use the 🔐 Cipher Decoder in Chapters 4 and 5?
Whenever you see an encrypted code snippet inside a message or unlocked vault, tap the 📤 Send to Decoder button right below it! Inside the 🔐 Decoder app, tap − Shift / + Shift (or ⇄ Reverse) until the output box turns into crisp readable English revealing the insider's badge or codename!
What happens if I accuse the wrong suspect?
You have 2 Arrest Warrants per case. A false accusation crosses that innocent employee off the roster and lets you return immediately to the workstation apps to double-check the logs and vault files before issuing your final warrant!
Does OS Sleuth work cleanly on narrow phone screens without tiny unreadable windows?
Yes! Instead of cramped overlapping desktop windows, ZinOS Forensics uses a clean mobile-first app dock with crisp high-contrast dark mode typography, large tap targets, and instant app switching on Android, iOS, and Chromebooks.
