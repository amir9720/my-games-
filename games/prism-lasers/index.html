How to Play Prism & Lasers (500 Levels)

Prism & Lasers is a sleek dark-mode optical logic game with 500 handcrafted and procedurally verified puzzles. Guide beams from the laser emitter into every crystal target on the grid—matching each target's exact light color—while listening to harmonic musical notes triggered by every reflection.

  • One-Tap Controls: Choose Mirror or Prism from the tray below the board and tap any empty square to place it. Tap the same piece once to flip its angle (/ to \), and tap again to pick it back up into your tray.
  • How Prisms Refract Light: A Prism acts as an optical splitter—one beam passes straight through while a second beam reflects at 90 degrees. When a White laser enters a Prism, it splits into a straight Blue beam and a reflected Red beam.
  • Color Filters & Light Mixing: Colored rings on the grid tint any passing laser into Red, Green, or Blue. When two different colored lasers cross inside a crystal target, their colors add together (Red + Blue = Magenta, Red + Green = Yellow, Green + Blue = Cyan).

Game FAQ

Why isn't a colored target lighting up when a White beam hits it?
Each crystal target requires its exact color frequency. Use a Prism or a colored lens filter on the board to convert White light into the exact color shown on the target.
Are all 500 optical levels verified to be solvable?
Yes. Every single level from 1 to 500 is verified by our live ray-tracer simulation before loading, ensuring every tool in your tray has a clean solution path.
