What Is Prism & Lasers? A Dark-Mode Optical Reflection Puzzle Online

Prism & Lasers is a free laser mirror puzzle game online engineered for fans of crisp geometric logic, dark-mode aesthetics, and real additive light physics. Set inside a minimalist obsidian laboratory grid, each level fires one or more glowing laser beams from fixed emitters. Your task is to tap rotatable 45-degree silvered mirrors and semi-transparent beam-splitter prisms until every faceted target crystal on the board receives the exact color of light it requires.

Built as a lightweight, no-download optical puzzle for phones, tablets, and Chromebooks, Prism & Lasers runs smoothly inside any modern browser with zero installation, zero timers, and automatic private progress saving across all 500 verified grids. Every mirror turn plays a soft crystalline harmonic ping through the Web Audio API, making it equally satisfying as a quiet late-night brain teaser or a focused STEM optics warm-up.

Additive RGB Laser Mixing Guide (Chapters 3–5 Strategy)

While paint pigments mix subtractively, real light beams follow additive RGB color physics—meaning two different colored laser rays converging onto the same target crystal combine into a brighter secondary hue! Mastering the table below will help you breeze through Chapters 3, 4, and 5:

Incoming Laser Beams Combined Crystal Color How to Achieve It
Red + Green Yellow Guide a Red beam and a Green beam into the same target gem from two open sides.
Red + Blue Magenta Route a Red ray and a Blue ray (via mirrors or color lenses) onto the Magenta gem.
Green + Blue Cyan Merge Green and Blue rays together—or split a Cyan emitter through a beam splitter!
Red + Green + Blue Pure White Combine all three primary channels (or Cyan + Red / Yellow + Blue) onto the diamond target.

Pro Tip: Work backward from each target gem! Look at which open corridors lead into a crystal—especially if obsidian walls surround three of its sides—then trace back to find the exact mirror orientation required to feed that lane.

Prism & Lasers FAQ

What is the difference between a solid mirror and a dashed beam-splitter prism?
A solid silvered mirror (/ or \) reflects 100% of the laser beam by 90 degrees. A semi-transparent beam splitter (dashed glowing diamond line) lets one beam pass straight through and bounces a second beam 90 degrees at the same time!
Why isn't my Magenta or Yellow crystal lighting up when a single beam hits it?
Secondary colored crystals require two primary colors combined at once! A Yellow gem needs both Red + Green rays, Magenta needs Red + Blue, and Cyan needs Green + Blue. Check the RGB table above for every combination.
Can two laser beams cross over the same empty square safely?
Yes! Just like real photons of light, perpendicular laser rays pass straight through each other across empty tiles without blocking or interfering—they only mix when they strike the same target crystal.
Does Prism & Lasers save my progress on mobile and Chromebook without login?
Yes. All solved grids across Levels 1 to 500 are saved automatically inside your browser's private local storage with zero accounts or downloads required.
