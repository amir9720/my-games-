Defusal Field Manual v4.2

Verify Serial (SN), Batteries (🔋), and Lit LEDs before acting.

1. Wire Array

Check the total number of wires and follow the first rule that applies:

If 4 Wires:
  1. More than 1 Red wire AND last SN digit is ODD → Cut the last Red wire.
  2. Last wire is Yellow AND NO Red wires → Cut the 1st wire.
  3. Exactly 1 Blue wire → Cut the 1st wire.
  4. More than 1 Yellow wire → Cut the last wire.
  5. Otherwise → Cut the 2nd wire.
If 5 Wires:
  1. Last wire is Black AND last SN digit is ODD → Cut the 4th wire.
  2. Exactly 1 Red wire AND more than 1 Yellow wire → Cut the 1st wire.
  3. NO Black wires → Cut the 2nd wire.
  4. Otherwise → Cut the 1st wire.
If 6 Wires:
  1. NO Yellow wires AND last SN digit is ODD → Cut the 3rd wire.
  2. Exactly 1 Yellow wire AND more than 1 White wire → Cut the 4th wire.
  3. NO Red wires → Cut the last wire.
  4. Otherwise → Cut the 4th wire.

2. Pressure Button

Follow the first matching rule:

  1. Button is BLUE and says ABORT → HOLD.
  2. More than 1 battery AND says DETONATE → QUICK TAP.
  3. Button is WHITE and GRN LED is lit → HOLD.
  4. 3 batteries AND RED LED is lit → QUICK TAP.
  5. Button is YELLOW → HOLD.
  6. Button is RED and says HOLD → QUICK TAP.
  7. Otherwise → HOLD.
If Holding (Check Side Light Strip):
  • BLUE strip: Release when timer shows a 4 anywhere.
  • YELLOW strip: Release when timer shows a 5 anywhere.
  • RED strip: Release when timer shows a 1 anywhere.
  • WHITE strip: Release when timer shows a 0 anywhere.

3. Keypad Cipher

Find the single column below containing all 4 symbols on the keypad. Press the 4 buttons in top-to-bottom order:

4. Logic Switches

Flip each switch to UP (1) or DOWN (0), then tap SYNC:

  • Switch A: UP if Serial (SN) contains a vowel (A, E, I, O, U), else DOWN.
  • Switch B: UP if RED LED is lit OR Batteries = 3, else DOWN.
  • Switch C: UP if BLU LED is lit AND last SN digit is EVEN, else DOWN.
  • Switch D: UP if GRN LED is lit AND Batteries ≥ 2, else DOWN.

How to Play Manual Bomb Defusal (500 Missions)

Manual Bomb Defusal puts your calm under pressure to the ultimate test. Every briefcase comes with a unique serial number (SN), battery count, and three colored LED indicators. Before cutting a single wire or tapping a button, cross-reference those hardware specs with the built-in Defusal Field Manual v4.2.

  • Mobile & Desktop Ready: On desktop screens, the briefcase and manual appear side by side. On smartphones, tap the Bomb Case and Defusal Manual tabs—your live timer, serial code, batteries, and LEDs stay pinned at the top so you never lose track.
  • Master the 4 Modules: Start with Wire Arrays in Chapter 1, then unlock Pressure Buttons, Keypad Ciphers, and Logic Switches across 500 escalating missions.
  • Watch Your Strikes: Early training missions allow 3 strikes before detonation, while Chapter 5 (Zero Margin) gives you just 1 strike and a tight countdown!

Game FAQ

How does "Holding" the Pressure Button work on mobile?
Press and keep your finger on the round button for half a second until the vertical light strip on its right glows Blue, Yellow, Red, or White. Keep holding until the matching digit (4, 5, 1, or 0) appears anywhere in the countdown timer, then lift your finger!
Which digit is the "last SN digit" in the serial number?
Look at the numeric digits inside the serial code (for example, in K4-82A, the final digit is 2, which is EVEN; in M7-39E, the final digit is 9, which is ODD).
