Panic causes strikes—calm checklists save lives! Before snipping a single wire or pushing a button, scan the briefcase's top edgework bar (`SN`, `🔋`, and `● LED`), then apply the exact logic rule for each module:

🎚️ Tactical DIP Switches (Ch. 4–5) Flip SW1–SW4 to ON or OFF using the briefcase's Vowel, Battery, Even-Digit, and LED rules, then tap VERIFY. Double-check vowels (A, E, I, O, U) inside the Serial Number—letters like Y are not vowels!

Manual Bomb Defusal FAQ

How do I perform a "Quick Tap" vs. a "Hold" on the Pressure Button?
For a Quick Tap, simply click or tap and release the big button immediately (under 0.3 seconds). For a Hold, press and keep holding until the vertical LED strip beside the button lights up Blue, Yellow, Red, or White—then release the instant the digital countdown clock displays the matching digit (4, 5, or 1)!
Does the countdown timer start before I've even looked at the briefcase?
No! The countdown clock stays cleanly armed and frozen until you interact with a module or open the Field Manual—and you can tap ⏸ Pause anytime if you need a breather mid-case.
What happens if I cut the wrong wire or press a symbol out of order?
Each mistake triggers a sharp buzzer and adds 1 Strike (Chapters 1–4 allow up to 3 strikes; Chapter 5 allows 2). A single strike doesn't end the case immediately—check the manual carefully and complete the remaining modules before your final strike!
Can two players play Manual Bomb Defusal together on separate phones?
Yes! Have Player 1 stay on the 💣 Bomb Casing tab while Player 2 opens the 📖 EOD Field Manual tab on their own device and talks Player 1 through each module—just like a real bomb squad team!
