How to Play Oobleck Maze (500 Levels)

Oobleck Maze simulates real-world shear-thickening non-Newtonian fluid—just like a bowl of cornstarch and water. Unlike ordinary maze games where speed wins, rushing here backfires immediately: applying sudden force turns the soft liquid around your heavy steel ball into a rigid crystalline solid.

  • Mobile & Mouse Tether Controls: Touch and drag anywhere on the maze to gently pull the steel sphere toward your finger or cursor. Keep the glowing tether line short and teal to glide smoothly through turns. Stretching the tether too far or jerking quickly pushes the Shear Meter past the white line and freezes you solid!
  • Keyboard Controls: On desktop, you can also use WASD or Arrow keys to steer calmly—watch out for slamming walls or rapid reversals inside dense puddles.
  • Dense Puddles & Timed Gates: Deep violet puddles thicken 55% faster than normal fluid, so slow down to a crawl as you cross them. Amber pressure gates open and close on a steady rhythm—wait patiently rather than darting through, or the fluid will lock you right at the threshold.

Game FAQ

How do I unfreeze when the Oobleck turns solid?
Simply lift your finger (or stop pulling hard) for less than a second. As soon as shear stress drops, the crystalline crust melts smoothly back into liquid so you can continue gliding.
Are all 500 mazes verified to have a clean path?
Yes. Every single level from 1 to 500 is deterministically generated and verified with a shortest-path solver—tap 💡 Calm Path anytime to illuminate the optimal route.
