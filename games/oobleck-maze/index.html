What Is Oobleck Maze? A Non-Newtonian Fluid Physics Game Online

Oobleck Maze is a free non-Newtonian fluid physics puzzle game online inspired by the famous science classroom mixture of cornstarch and water. Unlike ordinary liquids, oobleck is a shear-thickening dilatant fluid: punch it fast and its suspended starch grains lock together instantly like solid concrete; push your finger into it gently and it parts like smooth cream! Oobleck Maze turns that mind-bending real-world physics rule into a tactile 60 FPS labyrinth challenge across 500 progressive chemistry lab stages.

Built as a lightweight, no-download STEM physics game for smartphones, iPads, and Chromebooks, Oobleck Maze features intuitive one-thumb analog steering—drag a short distance to creep smoothly in 💧 LIQUID Mode, or pull further to sprint in 🪨 SOLID Mode—complete with procedural Web Audio squishy liquid warbles, crystalline solid bounces, and lab portal chimes.

Oobleck Maze FAQ

How do I control speed accurately on a smartphone touchscreen?
Touch and drag anywhere on the maze canvas—a subtle tether appears showing your steering direction and thrust! Keep your finger close to where you touched for gentle 💧 LIQUID (Slow) gliding, or drag further outward to surge into 🪨 SOLID (Fast) skimming!
How do keyboard controls work on Chromebooks and PCs?
Use WASD or Arrow Keys to glide steadily in 💧 LIQUID (Slow) speed—perfect for slipping right through Magenta Gel Curtains! Hold Shift (or Space) while moving to boost instantly into 🪨 SOLID (Fast) speed for crossing Oobleck Pits!
Why did I bounce off the Pink Gel Curtain when I tried to cross it?
Pink Gel Curtains harden instantly on high-speed impact! Watch the Viscosity Bar above the canvas—slow down until it reads 💧 LIQUID (Slow), then roll gently straight through the curtain like warm butter.
Is Oobleck Maze based on real-world fluid science?
Yes! Cornstarch-and-water oobleck is a real non-Newtonian dilatant suspension whose viscosity increases sharply under high shear stress—allowing people to run across a pool of oobleck yet sink if they stand still.
