What Is Controlled Demolition? A Structural Implosion Physics Game Online

Controlled Demolition is a free building demolition physics game online that puts you in the hard hat of a master explosives engineer. Rather than recklessly blowing up blocks in every direction, real urban demolition requires surgical precision: you must study each weathered concrete tower's center of mass, attach a limited budget of dynamite charges to key load-bearing columns, and hit DETONATE so the entire structure folds straight inward below the Target Clearance Line without grazing the gleaming emerald glass neighbor buildings flanking the construction site.

Powered by live 2D rigid-body simulation (Matter.js) and procedural deep-bass Web Audio blasts and crumbling concrete audio, Controlled Demolition runs smoothly at 60 FPS across phones, tablets, and Chromebooks with zero downloads and automatic private local storage saves across all 500 demolition sites.

Real Demolition Engineering Techniques (How to Implode Straight Inward)

Why does a tall concrete skyscraper topple sideways like a felled tree in one attempt, yet pancake neatly inside its own footprint in the next? Mastering these four real-world structural implosion tactics will let you clear even tight Chapter 5 downtown lots on your first blast:

Implosion Technique Best Site Layout How to Wire Your Charges
1. Symmetrical Pancake Drop Balanced tower flanked by protected buildings on both sides. Blast every ground-floor pillar simultaneously (0.0s) so gravity drops the upper floors straight down and crushes mid-level columns!
2. Center-First Inward Fold (Ch. 3–5) Wide 3-column or 4-column skyscrapers wedged tightly between two neighbors. Set the center support pillar(s) to 0.0s and the outer edge columns to 0.4s—the collapsing core pulls the outer walls safely inward!
3. Directional Tree-Fell One neighbor sits close on one side while the opposite side is an open lot. Place charges primarily on the open-lot side so the building leans cleanly away from the protected glass tower!
4. Upper Wing Counter-Blast (Ch. 4–5) Asymmetric stepped towers with a tall overhang on one side. Blast the upper wing's supports at 0.0s so the overhanging mass drops early before blasting the ground piers at 0.4s!

Want a quick structural consultation on a tricky tight-lot skyscraper? Tap 💡 Blast Plan anytime before detonating—our engineering guide highlights the exact recommended pillars and optimal fuse delays directly on the tower in glowing gold.

Controlled Demolition FAQ

How do I win a level in Controlled Demolition?
Plant dynamite charges on load-bearing pillars, press 🧨 DETONATE CHARGES, and wait roughly 2.5 seconds for the rubble to settle. If 100% of the target tower's debris lies below the dashed Target Clearance Line and zero protected neighbor buildings were struck, you clear the site!
Why do upper pillars crush automatically after I blast the ground floor?
Just like real-world progressive collapse ("pancaking"), when massive upper concrete slabs drop rapidly onto weakened vertical columns below, the impact force shatters those brittle columns into compact rubble—saving you dynamite!
How do timed delay fuses (0.0s, 0.4s, 0.8s) work in Chapters 3–5?
Select a fuse timing button below the site before tapping a pillar. For wide towers sandwiched between two neighbors, wire the inner center pillars to 0.0s and the outer pillars to 0.4s so the structure folds inward instead of spilling outward!
Can I adjust or remove a dynamite charge before pressing Detonate?
Yes! Tap any armed pillar again to disarm and reclaim that TNT charge, or tap Clear TNT to remove all planted charges before blasting.
