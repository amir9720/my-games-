How to Play Controlled Demolition (500 Jobs)

Controlled Demolition is a structural engineering physics game powered by Matter.js. Each city block features an aging high-rise surrounded by protected neighboring landmarks—such as hospitals, schools, museums, and cafes. Your objective is to bring the entire tower down below the dashed Green Clearance Line without letting a single falling beam strike a neighbor.

  • The Secret of Real Inward Implosions: On wide 3-column and 4-column towers, wire the center support columns to Wave T1 (0.0s) and the outer columns to Wave T2 (0.35s). When the core vanishes first, the floor slabs fold inward toward the middle before dropping straight down!
  • Tap to Wire & Edit: Select a timing wave (T1, T2, or T3) and tap any light concrete column to attach dynamite. Tap an already-wired column to update its wave or remove it. Dark riveted steel columns cannot be wired directly—bring them down by collapsing the floors underneath.
  • Quick Tweak Retries: If your first blast tips slightly sideways or stays too tall, tapping Edit & Retry rebuilds the tower with your previous charges still attached so you can adjust just one pillar or delay in seconds.

Game FAQ

Why did my building tip sideways into a neighboring hospital?
Blowing columns unevenly—like blasting only the left pillar first—removes support on one side and makes gravity lever the upper floors outward. Use simultaneous blasts on 2-column towers or center-first timed waves on wider towers.
How does the Blueprint Hint (💡) work?
Tapping Blueprint Hint overlays golden wave numbers (1 or 2) onto the exact load-bearing pillars verified to fold the tower neatly inward beneath the green clearance line.
