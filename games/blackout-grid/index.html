How to Play Blackout Grid Operator (500 Shifts)

Step into the real-time dispatch room of a modern electric utility. Real power grids must balance electricity generation and consumption every single second—too little supply drags frequency below 50.0 Hz toward a citywide brownout, while too much unchecked solar or wind drives frequency above 50.0 Hz toward an overload trip. Survive all 24 hours from 00:00 to 24:00 without letting Grid Stability hit 0%.

  • Read the 24-Hour Forecast Curve: The dashed amber line on the SCADA monitor previews upcoming city demand and weather anomalies—like midday Cloud Fronts ☁️, Wind Lulls 💨, and evening Stadium Surges ⚽. Ramp up generators 1–2 hours before a spike arrives!
  • Master the Battery Bank (🔋): During sunny midday hours when Solar surges, switch your Battery to Charge to soak up excess megawatts (keeping frequency safely at 50 Hz) and store free energy. Then flip it to Boost during the steep 18:00–21:00 evening rush!
  • Handle Breaker Trips (Chapter 4+): Severe storm shifts can occasionally trip your Gas Peaker breaker offline. Watch for the flashing red ⚡ RESET BREAKER button—tap it immediately and lean on Battery Boost while the turbine spins back up.

Game FAQ

Why is my frequency shooting over 51.5 Hz at noon?
Midday solar output can temporarily exceed city demand! Lower your Gas Peaker throttle, switch your Battery Bank to ⚡ Charge to absorb the surplus, or tap Solar / Wind Curtail to safely shed excess power.
What does the Grid Advisor (💡) button do?
Grid Advisor analyzes your exact live megawatt deficit or surplus and gives you a real-time dispatch recommendation—telling you precisely which turbine or battery mode will pull frequency back to 50.00 Hz.
