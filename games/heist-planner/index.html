How to Play Heist Blueprint Planner (500 Heists)

Heist Blueprint Planner blends architectural stealth planning with crisp turn-by-turn timing across 500 verified museum blueprints. Every single heist plays out in two thrilling phases: first plot your entire escape route beat-by-beat on the live blueprint, then press ▶ EXECUTE HEIST to watch your plan unfold in real time.

  • Live Beat Preview While Planning: As you tap or drag onto adjacent floor tiles, the blueprint advances its security preview to your exact planned beat (t). Toggle 👁 Peek t+1 anytime to see where lasers, cameras, and guards will be on the next beat before you step!
  • Master the Tactical Wait (⏱ WAIT +1): Tap the thief's current tile—or press ⏱ WAIT (+1)—to hold your position for one beat while a red laser blinks off, a CCTV cone rotates past, or a patrolling guard turns the corner.
  • Fast Blueprint Iteration: If an alarm trips during execution, your plotted route stays on the board when you tap Edit Plan. Simply undo a step or two, slip in a timed wait, and execute again until you nail a ghost getaway!

Game FAQ

Are all 500 museum blueprints guaranteed to have a valid stealth route?
Yes! Every blueprint from Heist #1 to #500 is mathematically verified with a Space-Time Breadth-First Search solver that accounts for pulsing lasers, sweeping cameras, and moving guard flashlights.
What happens if I walk directly through a patrolling guard?
Guards detect you if you step into their flashlight beam, land on their tile, or try to swap places with them head-on in a narrow hallway. Duck into an alcove or wait one beat to pass behind their back!
