What Is Heist Blueprint Planner? A Turn-Based Stealth Tactics Game Online

Heist Blueprint Planner is a free stealth heist strategy puzzle game online inspired by tactical classics like Door Kickers, Hitman GO, and Monaco, crafted natively in sleek 60 FPS HTML5 Canvas with zero downloads. Before your phantom operative ever sets foot inside the museum gallery, you lay out the entire infiltration route step by step across an illuminated cyan architectural floorplan. Every tap extends your dotted golden path by one synchronized beat (T = 1, 2, 3...)—and the blueprint instantaneously previews the exact future positions of patrolling security guards, rotating CCTV cones, and pulsing crimson laser barriers at that exact beat!

Built as a sharp, no-download tactical brain teaser for smartphones, iPads, and Chromebooks, Heist Blueprint Planner pairs calm, timer-free planning with crisp procedural Web Audio drafting pencil ticks, subtle spy footsteps, terminal hacking beeps, diamond loot shimmers, and sleek victory chords. Every cleared heist across all 500 blueprints saves automatically inside private browser local storage.

Heist Blueprint Planner FAQ

How do I know where guards and lasers will be when my spy reaches a tile?
Whenever you add a step (or tap ⏳ Wait 1 Beat) in Plan Mode, the entire blueprint automatically previews exact guard locations, flashlight cones, camera sweeps, and laser states at your current end beat T—so you can plan every move with 100% precision!
Why is the "⏳ Wait 1 Beat" button so important?
Sometimes the fastest way past a patrolling guard or pulsing laser is standing quietly behind a wall for 1 or 2 beats until the beam sweeps past! You can tap ⏳ Wait 1 Beat, tap directly on your spy's current endpoint tile, or press Space on your keyboard.
Can I reach the Exit Door before grabbing all the Diamonds?
The green Exit Arch unlocks only once you've routed through every 💎 Diamond on the floorplan! Check the top 💎 Loot counter—once all diamonds are collected, step onto the green Exit tile and hit ▶ EXECUTE HEIST!
Are keyboard controls available on laptops and Chromebooks?
Yes! Use WASD or Arrow Keys to extend your stealth blueprint one tile at a time, Space to Wait 1 Beat, Backspace to Undo, and Enter to Execute your heist!
