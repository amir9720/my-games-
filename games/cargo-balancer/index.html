How to Play Cargo Wave Balancer (500 Voyages)

Cargo Wave Balancer challenges your instinct for weight distribution and timing on a live Matter.js buoyancy engine. As your harbor crane lowers shipping containers onto the cargo ship, the vessel physically dips deeper into the water and rolls with every passing ocean swell. Load the entire manifest and keep every container dry for 5 full seconds without tilting past 38°.

  • Mobile & Desktop Controls: Drag left or right anywhere across the sky to glide the overhead crane trolley—or use the ◀ / ▶ buttons and keyboard arrow keys. Tap ⟳ 90° (or press R) to stand a container upright when deck space gets tight, then tap DROP CARGO (or Space) to release the hooks.
  • Respect Heavy Steel (8t) Cargo: Dark steel containers weigh four times as much as light blue boxes! Place heavy 8t cargo directly near the centerline of the ship—or drop it on the high side to immediately level out a leaning deck.
  • Time the Waves: In Chapters 4 and 5, steep rolling swells rock the hull constantly. Release containers right as the deck swings level so they seat flat instead of sliding downhill!

Game FAQ

What causes a voyage to fail?
A voyage fails immediately if any shipping container splashes overboard into the ocean or if the cargo vessel lists beyond the 38° capsize limit.
How does the Balance Guide (💡) help?
Tapping Balance Guide calculates the ship's real-time weight distribution and displays a glowing golden drop marker showing the ideal horizontal spot to counteract any lean.
