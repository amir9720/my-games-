What Is Cargo Wave Balancer? A Ship Loading Physics Game Online

Cargo Wave Balancer is a free cargo ship balancing physics game online that puts you in command of a bustling harbor gantry crane over live ocean swells. Driven by real 2D rigid-body dynamics (Matter.js) paired with multi-point Archimedes hull buoyancy, every container you release onto the deck physically lowers the ship's draft and shifts its heel angle toward Port (left) or Starboard (right). Load your manifest unevenly—or drop a massive 24-ton steel box too far off-center—and the vessel rolls dangerously past its safe stability limit, sending containers tumbling into the sea!

Crafted as a lightweight, no-download maritime physics puzzle for smartphones, tablets, and Chromebooks, Cargo Wave Balancer runs smoothly at 60 FPS with responsive thumb-drag crane steering and procedural Web Audio metallic container clanks, splashes, and harbor departure horns. Every cleared manifest across all 500 voyages saves automatically inside private local storage.

Cargo Wave Balancer FAQ

Why did my voyage fail when the ship tilted too far sideways?
A real cargo freighter capsizes if its roll angle (List) exceeds 34°, or if any shipping container slides past the deck bulkheads into the ocean. Counterbalance heavy boxes immediately on the opposite side to bring the List back near 0°!
How do I stop cyan Reefer containers from sliding off the deck in Chapters 4 and 5?
Cyan Reefer boxes have frosted low-friction steel runners. Wedge them snugly between two high-friction standard boxes—or drop them on top of a flat, centered 40ft container—so ocean swells can't push them overboard!
What happens during the 5-second Sea Trial after the last box lands?
Once your final container settles on deck, ocean waves swell slightly stronger for 5 full seconds to test your stowage stability. Keep every box aboard and the hull upright to sound the harbor victory horn!
Can I control the port gantry crane with both touchscreens and keyboards?
Yes! Drag your finger left or right anywhere across the sky canvas (or tap ◀ Port / Stbd ▶) on mobile, or use A/D / Arrow Keys + Spacebar on desktop and Chromebooks.
