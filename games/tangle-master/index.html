What Is Tangle Master? A Tactile Rope Untangling Browser Puzzle

Tangle Master is a free rope untangling puzzle game online that recreates the deeply satisfying feel of sorting braided colored cords on a physical workshop pegboard. Every stage stretches vivid woven ropes between round metallic pins plugged into recessed socket holes. Because only a limited number of empty sockets exist on each board, untying a dense braid requires careful sequence planning—choosing which pin to park temporarily in an open slot so tangled ropes can pass cleanly past each other without overlapping.

Built from the ground up as a lightweight, no-download knot puzzle for mobile phones, iPads, and Chromebooks, Tangle Master supports both smooth fingertip dragging and effortless tap-to-move controls. Every peg snaps into place with a crisp wooden click synthesized live through the Web Audio API, and every rope that becomes completely tangle-free shimmers with a soft checkmark highlight until the entire board clears.

How to Untie Tough Rope Boards in Fewer Moves (Strategy Guide)

While early 3-rope boards untie in two or three quick swaps, later 6- and 7-rope boards with locked brass pins reward systematic thinking. Apply these four proven Tangle Master untangling strategies whenever a knot looks crowded:

Pegboard Strategy When to Use It How It Solves the Knot
1. Clear the Edge Ropes First Any board with 4+ ropes on twin bars or circular rings. Move one cord completely to the far left or right sockets so it turns clear and shrinks the remaining puzzle!
2. Use Spare Holes as Swap Buffers Two ropes need to trade exact sockets directly. Park Pin A in an empty buffer socket, slide Pin B into Pin A's old hole, then drop Pin A into Pin B's vacated slot.
3. Respect Locked Brass Pins Chapters 3–5 featuring hexagonal locked anchors. A locked pin dictates its rope's permanent side—pull its other movable end into an adjacent hole on that same side!
4. Untangle Top-to-Bottom Layers Dense 7-rope grand knots in Chapters 4 & 5. Target whichever rope currently has the highest number of red crossing sparks first to free multiple lanes at once.

Need a quick nudge on a tight board? Tap 💡 Rope Hint—our solver automatically finds an exact legal move (including smart swap-buffer steps when target holes are full!) and highlights both the pin to lift and the open socket to drop it into in glowing gold.

Tangle Master FAQ

How is Tangle Master different from Tension & Strings?
In Tension & Strings, every node connects to multiple neighbors and moves freely anywhere on the canvas. In Tangle Master, independent braided ropes snap strictly into discrete pegboard socket holes—making empty buffer sockets and move order the heart of the puzzle!
Can I play by tapping instead of dragging pins on mobile?
Yes! Tangle Master supports both play styles seamlessly. Drag any movable pin directly into an open glowing hole, or tap a pin to lift it and tap any empty socket to glide it over cleanly.
Are there move limits or game-over screens if I take extra moves?
No harsh game-overs or timers! We count your moves so you can challenge yourself for clean efficiency, and you can undo steps or reset a pegboard anytime for stress-free play.
Does Tangle Master save my solved boards without an account?
Yes. Your cleared boards across all 500 levels are automatically saved inside your browser's private local storage on mobile, tablet, or desktop.
