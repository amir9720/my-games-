How to Play Tangle Master (500 Levels)

Tangle Master is a tactile pegboard brain teaser built around thick braided ropes and discrete anchor sockets. Each colorful rope connects two round pins seated in the board. Whenever two ropes cross over each other, a subtle red knot marker glows at the crossing point. Reposition the pins into open sockets until every rope lies free and flat.

  • Drag or Tap Controls: Press and drag any movable pin directly onto an open circular socket—or tap a pin once to select it, then tap any empty socket to snap it cleanly into place.
  • Master Steel Anchors (Chapter 3+): Starting at Level 201, hexagonal steel-bolted pins lock one end of select ropes firmly to the pegboard. Plan your open sockets around those fixed anchors first!
  • Pro Strategy Tip: Move the ends of shorter ropes toward the outer edges of the board to free up central sockets, then route longer cords neatly alongside the perimeter.

Game FAQ

Are all 500 levels guaranteed to be solvable even with locked steel pins?
Yes. Every puzzle is constructed backwards from a verified zero-crossing pegboard layout with multiple empty sockets, ensuring there is always a clean sequence of valid moves to untie every knot.
What does the Untie Step (💡) button do?
Tapping Untie Step performs one verified move toward a knot-free layout—either placing a tangled pin directly into its ideal open socket or clearing space when two pins need to swap places.
