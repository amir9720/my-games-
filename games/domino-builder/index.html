How to Play Infinite Domino Builder (500 Cascades)

Infinite Domino Builder brings the addictive precision of domino rally art straight to your fingertips. Draw flowing lines, smooth spirals, multi-way splits, and crossover bridge loops across 500 puzzle arenas—or switch to 🛠️ Free Sandbox to sculpt massive 200+ domino masterpieces with four selectable color palettes.

  • Draw & Branch Effortlessly: Drag your finger or mouse starting near the glowing green START domino. Pieces auto-space themselves cleanly along your curve. Need to hit two or three 🔔 Goal Bells at once? Simply start a second stroke branching off the side of any existing domino in your trail!
  • Build Figure-8 Overpass Loops (Chapter 3+): Wooden Bridge Ramps let your trail cross right over itself! Draw straight lengthwise across a bridge to elevate dominoes onto the upper deck, and draw sideways through its arch to pass cleanly underneath without premature collisions.
  • Avoid Fragile Red Pillars (Chapter 4+): Keep your curves safely away from glowing red glass hazards—if any falling tile smacks a red pillar, the run resets cleanly to Edit Mode with all your dominoes preserved so you can touch up that corner and try again!

Game FAQ

Why did my domino chain stop halfway around a sharp corner?
Just like real wooden dominoes, a 180° hairpin bend can miss the next tile! Draw gentle, sweeping arcs (or use 🧹 Erase to smooth out a tight kink) and watch the whole line cascade effortlessly.
Can I watch my domino cascade in slow motion?
Yes! Tap the 1.0× Pace button next to Topple to cycle between cinematic 0.5× Slow-Mo, standard 1.0×, and turbo 2.0× cascade speeds—even live while tiles are falling!
