How to Play Fold & Reveal (500 Origami Levels)

Fold & Reveal is a crisp spatial layering puzzle inspired by traditional Japanese paper folding. Each stage spreads an unfolded sheet of colorful washi paper across the crafting desk, with painted shapes, ribbons, and secret message badges printed on its flaps. Your goal is to fold every flap inward in the exact bottom-to-top sequence needed to recreate the Target Artwork.

  • Tap or Swipe to Fold: Tap any open edge or corner flap—or swipe it smoothly toward the center square—to crease it flat over the paper base. Look closely at the faint print-through on each open flap to recognize which motif it carries!
  • Layer Order Is Everything: Just like real paper, whichever flap you fold later covers the flaps underneath it. Inspect the Target Artwork in the top-left corner: find which colored flap sits completely uncovered on top (save it for last!) and which larger base flap sits tucked behind everything (fold it first!).
  • Master Corner Creases & Hidden Words: Chapter 2 introduces 45° diagonal corner folds, Chapter 3 weaves hidden secret words into the overlapping paper layers, and Chapters 4–5 combine up to 8 interlocking edge and corner flaps into intricate origami stars.

Game FAQ

What if I fold all flaps, but the picture doesn't match?
Simply tap the center paper (or tap ← Unfold) to peel back your last fold smoothly—no game-over penalties! You can also tap 💡 Fold Hint to glow the exact next flap in golden light.
If two flaps don't overlap each other, does their order matter?
No! Fold & Reveal compares the exact final assembled image against the Target Artwork, so any valid folding sequence that produces the exact same layered result is accepted instantly.
