How to Play Antique Restorer (500 Vintage Relics)

Step into a quiet wooden horologist's workshop and bring 500 forgotten treasures back to life—from Victorian pocket watches and maritime compasses to brass horn gramophones, twin-lens cameras, heirloom music boxes, and ship chronometers. Every restoration unfolds in three tactile, soothing stages:

  • Step 1 — Scrub Rust & Patina (🧽): Drag your brass wire brush back and forth across encrusted orange rust and green verdigris patina. In higher chapters, thicker corrosion takes a couple of steady passes to break free.
  • Step 2 — Assemble the Gear Train (⚙️): Once clean, the internal mechanism opens up. Pick each gear from the wooden workbench tray below and tap (or drag onto) the matching dashed axle outline by size and metal finish. Once the final gear clicks into place, the entire interlocking train whirrs to life!
  • Step 3 — Mirror Polish (✨): Buff away the dull matte oxidation film with your soft velvet cloth until the brass and gold gleam with sparkling highlights and a vintage chime rings out.

Game FAQ

How do I know which gear goes on which axle during Step 2?
Each empty axle displays a dashed circular outline matching the exact diameter and metallic color (Brass, Copper, Steel, or Gold) of the gear that belongs there. Tap 💡 Workshop Hint anytime to highlight the exact axle for your selected gear!
What if I miss one tiny speck of rust or tarnish?
Tap 💡 Workshop Hint during scrubbing or polishing to flash any remaining spots in bright glowing orange, and each stage auto-finishes cleanly at 96% so you never have to pixel-hunt.
