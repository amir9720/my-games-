What Is Antique Restorer? A Vintage Restoration ASMR Game Online

Antique Restorer is a free antique restoration simulator game online inspired by relaxing workshop repair videos and watchmaking craftsmanship. Spread out on a deep emerald jeweler's velvet mat are 500 tarnished museum heirlooms—ranging from Victorian gold pocket watches and brass nautical compasses to Art Deco lockets, ornate skeleton keys, vintage gramophones, and gilded royal music boxes. Each relic arrives heavily oxidized with green copper verdigris, dark iron rust, dull cloudy tarnish, and loose precision parts waiting to be brought back to showroom perfection.

Built as a lightweight, no-download ASMR craft game for smartphones, iPads, and Chromebooks, Antique Restorer runs natively at 60 FPS with live procedural Web Audio bristle brushing, velvety cloth polishing swooshes, crisp metallic gear clicks, and a triumphant antique music-box chime—zero countdown timers and automatic private browser saving across all 500 relics.

Antique Restorer FAQ

Do I need to manually switch tools between Scrubbing, Polishing, and Fitting Parts?
No—as soon as you clean 95% of the rust and verdigris, Antique Restorer automatically polishes the final specks with a chime and hands you the ✨ Velvet Cloth! Once mirror-buffed, your ⚙️ Tweezers activate automatically so you can snap the missing gears and gems into place.
How do I find a stubborn 4% spot of tarnish hiding on the brass?
Tap the 💡 Loupe Hint button right below the tool dock! Every remaining uncleaned or unpolished cell pulses bright neon gold for 3.5 seconds so you can buff it spotless instantly.
Why are there multiple identical-looking gears in Step 3?
In Chapters 3 through 5, heirlooms may hold both large brass drive wheels, smaller silver pinions, ruby jewels, or ornate hands! Match each part's size and shape to its glowing dotted socket—and tapping 💡 Loupe Hint draws a golden guide line from the next tray part straight to its exact slot.
Can I play Antique Restorer smoothly on phones and Chromebooks without an app?
Yes! Scrubbing, polishing, and dragging gears run at a smooth 60 FPS on Android Chrome, iOS Safari, and Chromebooks with synthesized Web Audio ASMR and automatic local browser saving.
