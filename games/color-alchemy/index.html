How to Play Color Alchemy Lab (500 Formulas)

Color Alchemy Lab uses real artist RYB subtractive pigment mixing—just like blending oil paints or watercolors on a palette—inside a glowing glass workshop. Above your mixing flask sits a split comparison medallion showing the Target Elixir on the left and Your Brew directly beside it on the right.

  • Intuitive Paint Physics: Combine Crimson (Red) + Amber (Yellow) for warm Orange, Amber + Cobalt (Blue) for vibrant Emerald Green, Crimson + Cobalt for Royal Purple, or blend all three primaries to craft rich earthy browns and muted olives.
  • Master Tints & Shades (Chapters 2–5): Starting at Formula #101, Pearl White unlocks soft pinks, sky blues, and creamy pastels. At Formula #201, Obsidian Black unlocks deep midnight navy, burgundy, and forest tones.
  • Refine with Undo & Ratios: Remember that color depends on the proportion of drops—adding a second drop of Yellow to a 1:1 Orange shifts it smoothly toward Golden Marigold. If your flask fills up, tap ← Undo Drop or ↺ Pour Out anytime to fine-tune your recipe!

Game FAQ

Is every one of the 500 target colors guaranteed to be 100% matchable?
Yes! Every level's target swatch is synthesized directly from a valid multi-drop pigment recipe within the flask's drop limit, meaning a 100% exact match is always achievable.
How does the Alchemist Hint (💡) button help?
Tapping Alchemist Hint inspects your current brew and glows a golden ring around the single best next pigment drop to bring your elixir closer to the target—or lets you know if undoing a misplaced drop will get you right back on track.
