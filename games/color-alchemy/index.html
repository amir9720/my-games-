What Is Color Alchemy Lab? An Authentic RYB Paint Mixing Game Online

Color Alchemy Lab is a free online paint color mixing game built for visual artists, designers, students, and puzzle fans who love tactile color matching challenges. Most casual web games average standard digital RGB hex codes—meaning blue and yellow turn into dull muddy gray! Color Alchemy Lab runs a true RYB subtractive pigment model inspired by real acrylic and gouache paint physics, so blending Blue + Yellow produces lush leaf green, Red + Yellow bursts into warm tangerine orange, and Red + Blue swirls into rich royal violet.

Crafted as a lightweight, no-download color theory brain teaser for smartphones, iPads, and Chromebooks, every tap sends a gleaming droplet splashing into your animated glass alchemy flask with gentle wave physics and soothing liquid bubbles synthesized live through the Web Audio API. Match all 500 unique pigment formulas at your own pace with zero countdown stress and automatic private local storage saves.

Color Alchemy Lab FAQ

Why does Blue + Yellow make bright Green here instead of gray?
Standard computer RGB averaging turns Blue + Yellow into gray because screens mix light, not paint. Color Alchemy Lab uses a real trilinear RYB subtractive paint model so mixing pigments behaves just like real acrylics and watercolors!
Does the order in which I add pigment drops change the final color?
No! Only the final ratio of drops inside your flask matters—for example, adding Red then Yellow produces the exact same vibrant Orange as adding Yellow then Red.
What happens if my flask fills up to the Drop Cap before reaching the match threshold?
You can tap ← Undo Drop to remove your last drop(s) and try a different pigment, or tap 🚿 Rinse Flask for a fresh empty beaker with zero penalties.
How does the Recipe Hint (💡) button help?
Recipe Hint compares your current flask contents against a verified 100%-match formula and glows the exact next pigment dropper bottle you should tap in bright gold!
