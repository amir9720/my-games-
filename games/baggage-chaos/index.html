How to Play Baggage Routing Chaos (500 Shifts)

Take command of the airport's automated baggage sorting deck across 500 escalating shifts. Suitcases roll out from the intake chutes at the top and travel down conveyor belts toward the departure gates below. Your sole control is flipping the circular track switches at each junction—one tap instantly redirects the active belt.

  • Read the Glowing Track: The bright glowing path leaving each circular switch shows where the next suitcase will turn. Inactive branches stay dimmed so you can scan the whole terminal in a split second.
  • Watch for Express Suitcases (⚡): Starting in Chapter 2, priority express bags marked with a lightning bolt travel 35% faster than standard luggage—prioritize their switches first!
  • Color + Letter Coding: Every suitcase and airplane gate displays both a distinct color and its first letter (R Red, B Blue, G Green, Y Yellow, P Purple, O Orange) for crisp visibility on mobile screens.

Game FAQ

Can I change a switch right before a suitcase reaches it?
Yes! The routing decision happens the exact moment a suitcase reaches the center of a switch junction. You can flip switches as fast as needed to separate back-to-back bags.
What happens when I tap the Hint button during a busy shift?
Hint scans the closest incoming suitcase and pulses a golden ring around the exact switch you need to flip so it reaches its matching gate.
